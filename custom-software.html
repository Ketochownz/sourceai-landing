How it works

Discover, design, build.

Step one

Discover

We map how the work actually happens today — the systems, the workarounds, the spreadsheets — before proposing anything.

Step two

Design

What that capability looks like

Two examples currently in progress.

Lucid DMS

In development

An AI-native dealer management system for New Zealand automotive groups — a full intelligence layer connecting the workshop, sales, parts and accounting, built for a real design-partner dealership.

01

Workshop Kanban

Runs the service department from booking to invoice on a real-time board. Job cards can be created automatically by an AI voice agent.

02

AI voice booking

Answers inbound service calls, confirms the vehicle by rego, and drops a populated job card on the board before the workshop opens.

03

Recon & sales

Auto-generates recon task lists and cost budgets, plus deal jackets with a live gross-profit calculator as finance and accessories are added.

04

Parts & accounting

Bulk parts catalogue imports, plus vehicle sales and recon costs pushed straight to the correct mapped accounting ledger — no manual bookkeeping.

CarTrader

In build

A wholesale marketplace for the trade-in every dealer doesn't want to keep. List an off-brand vehicle once, get offers from matching dealers, pick the best price without a single phone call.

01

List once

A dealer lists a car with photos and a condition report in a few minutes.

02

Matching dealers notified

Every dealer with a matching saved filter gets an instant notification — no group chats, no phone chains.

03

Blind offers

Interested buyers submit offers independently, without seeing what anyone else has bid.

04

Seller picks the best

The seller sees every offer ranked and can accept whenever they're ready — no auction pressure.

Common questions

What clients usually ask.

Do I need a working prototype already?

No — most projects start from a problem and a rough idea of the workflow, not a finished spec. We'll help shape it during discovery.

What does a custom build cost?

It depends entirely on scope — get in touch and we'll walk through what you need before putting a number on it.

Will it connect to the systems I already use?

Usually, yes — integrating with existing accounting, CRM or industry-specific systems is a normal part of these builds, not an afterthought.
