Lucid Chat Agent

Flagship · Live with a real dealer group

A bespoke, agentic AI chat agent built specifically for automotive dealerships, specialising in lead generation and FAQ handling — not a generic FAQ bot with a car dealership skin. It knows every brand's specs and pricing, checks real stock against your own dealer system, looks up a customer's plate for service or parts, and pushes every lead straight into your sales pipeline.

01

Live stock, not a script

Connects directly to your dealer management system so answers reflect what's actually on the lot right now, synced automatically.

02

Numberplate lookup

A customer's rego resolves straight to their vehicle's make, model and details inline — no forms, no back-and-forth.

03

Per-brand knowledge

Every franchise gets its own agent with its own knowledge base — no VW customer accidentally getting Isuzu pricing.

04

Leads straight to your CRM

Every conversation becomes a qualified lead in your existing sales pipeline, tagged by source, ready for a salesperson to pick up.

05

Books test drives and service appointments directly in the conversation — no phone tag, no missed follow-up.
